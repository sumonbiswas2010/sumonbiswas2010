## Selected Work

### EmployeeEcho: AI Interview Platform

`Node.js` `Microservices` `PostgreSQL` `OpenAI` `ZeptoMail`

AI platform that runs the full hiring loop: job and candidate intake, invitations, scheduling and reminders, live AI-conducted interviews, and structured candidate evaluation. I designed the interview algorithms and job-specific assessment workflows, along with caching for faster AI processing and camera-image verification for interview authenticity.

Live: [employeeecho.tech](https://employeeecho.tech)

### ChangeLogger: Shopify Public App

`Remix` `PostgreSQL` `Shopify GraphQL` `Webhooks`

Shopify App Store application that tracks changes across products, orders, collections, tags, and themes, giving merchants a real audit trail. Built the webhook processing and GraphQL integrations that capture store activity and persist searchable event history.

Live: [apps.shopify.com/tag-change-detector](https://apps.shopify.com/tag-change-detector)

### Revorium: Global Fintech Platform

`Node.js` `Microservices` `Next.js` `PostgreSQL` `AWS` `Stripe` `PayPal` `Revolut`

Backend services for real-time transfers, international payments, invoicing, and payouts across web and mobile. Transaction services run on AWS (ECS, RDS, IAM, SES for transactional alerts), with Stripe, PayPal, and Revolut mapped to distinct financial workflows.

Company: [linkedin.com/company/revorium](https://ee.linkedin.com/company/revorium)

### AdsPlanner: AI Ad Campaign Generation Platform

`Puppeteer` `RabbitMQ` `OpenAI` `PostgreSQL` `AWS S3`

SaaS platform that scrapes any brand or product URL through queued Puppeteer workers, then generates ad campaign images and copy from the extracted content. I designed the image generation algorithms and the S3-backed bulk media pipeline that produces creatives at scale across platform dimensions.

Live: [adsplanner.ai](https://adsplanner.ai)
